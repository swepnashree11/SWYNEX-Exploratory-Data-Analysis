TASK 2: EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS
Project: Cafe Sales - Dirty Data for Cleaning Training

1. DATASET OVERVIEW
The dataset contains 10,000 transactions and 8 columns.
The data covers cafe sales transactions from January to December 2023.

2. KEY STATISTICS
Total Transactions: 10,000
Total Revenue: 88,952
Average Transaction Value: 8.93
Median Transaction Value: 8.00
Average Quantity per Transaction: 3.03

3. USEFUL INSIGHTS

Insight 1: Best-Selling Revenue Category
Salad generated the highest revenue of 17,320,
followed by Sandwich at 13,664.
Cookie generated the lowest revenue of 3,223
among identified item categories.

Insight 2: Payment Method Preference
Among known payment methods, Digital Wallet had
the highest transaction count with 2,291 transactions.
However, 3,178 transactions had an Unknown payment method,
so the payment preference analysis has limitations.

Insight 3: Sales by Location
Among known locations, In-store generated revenue
of 27,127, while Takeaway generated 26,487.50.
Takeaway had 3,022 transactions and In-store had 3,017.
Their sales performance was very similar.

Insight 4: Monthly Revenue Trend
June recorded the highest monthly revenue at 7,350.
February recorded the lowest monthly revenue at 6,633.50.
Monthly revenue remained relatively consistent throughout 2023.

Insight 5: Sales Outliers
The IQR method identified 268 potential outlier transactions.
The upper outlier limit was 24, and the maximum transaction
value was 25. These may be valid high-value purchases.

Insight 6: Missing Data
The dataset contained 38 missing Quantity values,
38 missing Price Per Unit values, 40 missing Total Spent values,
and 460 missing Transaction Date values.
These missing values should be considered when interpreting results.

5. CONCLUSION
The exploratory analysis shows that Salad was the highest
revenue-generating identified item, monthly revenue was
relatively stable, and In-store and Takeaway sales were similar.
The analysis also identified potential outliers and missing
data limitations that should be considered in business decisions.
